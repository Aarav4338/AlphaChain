## Slide 17

HACKATHON PROBLEM STATEMENT

Rules and Constraints

No market-making and arbitrage strategies, only directional. 

Long and Short (1x only) are both available.

No leverage. 

Teams are required to submit repositories for judging and validation. 

Bots should demonstrate autonomous trade execution aligned with their declared strategy for at least 10 trading days. 

We'll provide each team an AWS sub-account to launch an EC2 instance for hosting your bot on cloud. 

100k starting portfolio for each team. 

Each executed order takes 0.1% commission fee for taker order (market order) and 0.05% for maker order (limit order).
